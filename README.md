HR Analytics Dashboard , project overview
Organizations often struggle to understand why employees leave the company, which departments experience higher attrition, and what workforce factors impact employee retention. High employee turnover increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and affects overall business performance.
The goal of this project is to analyze HR data and build an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard that helps management identify the major causes of employee attrition and workforce trends.

Using data visualization and analytics, the dashboard provides insights into:

•	Overall employee count 
•	Employee attrition and attrition rate 
•	Average employee age and salary 
•	Attrition by education background 
•	Attrition by age group 
•	Attrition by gender 
•	Attrition by salary slab 
•	Attrition by years at company 
•	Attrition by job role 

These insights help HR teams and business managers:

•	Identify high-risk employee groups 
•	Understand which job roles have the highest turnover 
•	Analyze the relationship between salary and attrition 
•	Improve employee retention strategies 
•	Support workforce planning and decision-making
